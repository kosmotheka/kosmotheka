Panel that slides in from an edge. On mobile the app uses it from the bottom for short confirmations and choices (rounded top corners, a grab handle, content and two full-width buttons), and from the left for the navigation drawer. On desktop the same content goes in a `Dialog`. Compose `Sheet` > `SheetContent side="bottom"` > `SheetHeader` (`SheetTitle`, `SheetDescription`), body, `SheetFooter`.
