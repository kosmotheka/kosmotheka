The app searches public catalogs on the internet for you (they are listed in [Where the details come from](../data-sources/)). Each one knows different things and searches in its own way. These tips help you find what you are looking for at the first try.

## Best of all: the code

If it has a barcode or an ISBN, **scan it or type the number** instead of the title. The number says exactly which edition it is, and the details arrive complete: publisher, year, cover. By title, every edition shows up and you have to pick.

If the barcode is a shop label, on the phone use "Photo of the number": it reads the ISBN printed on the back cover. It is explained in [Add a book](../add-a-book/).

## First, the type

Before typing, pick what you are looking for: Book, Comic, Board game, Film… **Each type searches its own catalogs.** A comic searched as a book, or a film as a TV series, may not show up. If you cannot find it, try the other type it could be: a manga or a graphic novel can be filed as a book or as a comic, depending on the catalog.

## What to type

- **Only the title**, without the author. Several catalogs (the National Library of Spain and the Library of Congress) only look at the title: if you add the author, they find nothing.
- **Without the subtitle**, or only its beginning. "The Name of the Wind" finds more than "The Name of the Wind: The Kingkiller Chronicle, Day One".
- **As it appears on the cover**, with whole words.
- **If it is a translation**, try the original title too. Some catalogs only have the edition in their own language.
- **At least two letters.** Results appear as you type.

## In which order they come

For books, the ones called exactly what you typed and in the app's language come first; then the ones that contain those words; the rest at the end. The editions of one work are together. For the other types, they come in the order each catalog answers.

Each result says which catalog it comes from. If two look alike, the one with more details (cover, publisher, year) is usually the right one.

## If little or nothing shows up

- **See how many catalogs that type has.** In "Settings", "Data providers", each type says something like "2 of 5". If the number is orange, the app searches very few places: for films, video games and comics, with no key and no account only Wikidata is left, and it knows little about editions. With a free key of your own, or an account on a server that offers them, TMDB, RAWG and Comic Vine are used. Board games by title and role-playing books, with BoardGameGeek and RPGGeek, only work with an account.
- **Check the catalog is not turned off** on that same screen.
- **See if there is a notice** under the search box: a catalog that does not answer (because of the connection or because it went over its limit) says so there. Try again in a while.
- **Try fewer words.** One misspelled word is enough for it not to show up.

## If it shows up, but with details missing or wrong

Tap the result anyway: the page opens so you can check it before saving. You can correct or complete any detail, and the cover can be searched again (with the corrected ISBN, title and authors) or replaced with a photo of your own. It is in [Add a book](../add-a-book/#when-what-you-were-looking-for-appears).

## If it is in no catalog

It happens with old books, small editions, independent games or homemade things. Tap "Add manually" and type what you know: only the title is required. Anything without a type (stamps, coins, figures) goes in "Other", which does not search anywhere.

## Many things at once

To start a big catalog, on the phone try "Photo of a shelf": it reads the spines in a photo and looks each one up. On a computer, "Quick add" lets you enter many numbers one after another. Both are in [Add a book](../add-a-book/). And if you already have your list in a spreadsheet, it can be imported: see [Backups](../backups/).
