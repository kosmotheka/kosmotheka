This is where the guides for people who use the app will go: adding a book by scanning or searching, sharing the library over wifi or with an account, who can do what, backups, and what to do if you lose your phone. Each page will answer one concrete question, with screenshots and no technical terms.

No pages are written yet. In the meantime, the project's website has the essentials:

- [How it works](https://kosmotheka.com/en/faq.html), the frequently asked questions.
- [Downloads](https://kosmotheka.com/en/download.html), with what is already available.
- [Privacy policy](https://kosmotheka.com/en/privacy.html), on what leaves your device and what does not.
- [Delete your account](https://kosmotheka.com/en/delete-account.html).
