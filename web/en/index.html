Why it’s different

Made for home, not for the cloud.

01

No account, no internet

Everything is stored on your devices. An account is optional, keeps your libraries up to date from afar and, for now, is by invitation.

02

Shared at home

Phones and computers pair with a QR code and sync over your Wi-Fi, with no servers involved.

03

Several libraries

The family one, the role-playing club’s, your own. Each with its own people.

04

A role for everyone

Some people manage, some edit, and some just browse and ask for things to be added. Admins accept or decline.

05

Quick to add

Scan the ISBN or barcode and the details fill themselves in. Or search by title, or import your spreadsheet.

06

08

Everyone’s own notes

To read, reading, finished or dropped (or playing, watching…), stars, favourites and “I want this”.

Sharing at home

One QR code. Done.

No accounts to create, nothing to set up. Like sending a photo from one phone to another, but only once.

  1. Show the code

    On one phone, open the library and open the menu, go to “Sharing and devices” and tap “Pair another device”. A QR code appears.

  2. Scan it with the other

    With the other phone or computer, on the same Wi-Fi. Choose which libraries to share and with which role.

  3. That’s it

    From then on they pass changes along whenever they’re both at home. If one is away, it catches up when it gets back.

Privacy

Your data is yours.

What you add stays on your devices. We never see it, because it never reaches us.

  • No adsNot now, not later.
  • No trackingNo analytics, no cookies, in the app or on this site.
  • No account requiredOnly if you want your libraries up to date from afar. For now, the cloud is by invitation.
  • Backups whenever you likeKeep them wherever you prefer. And export to CSV.
  • Free softwareIt will be AGPL: the code, open for anyone to see.
  • Your own serverIf you fancy it, you’ll be able to run your own.
