# Trademark policy

The code of Kosmotheka is free software. Its **name and logos are not**: the AGPL and the MIT licence cover the code and the specification, not the "Kosmotheka" name or any of its logos. This page says what that means in practice.

## What this covers

- The name **Kosmotheka**.
- **Every logo**, in every colour (indigo, black, white, and the versions for dark backgrounds):
  - the **symbol**: the indigo square with a star that breaks into pixels;
  - the **logotype**: the word "Kosmotheka" as drawn in the logo;
  - the **horizontal** and **stacked** logos: the symbol together with the name;
  - the **app icons** made from the symbol.

You can download them, with how to use them, from the [name and logos page](https://docs.kosmotheka.com/en/run-your-server/name-and-logos/) of the documentation. The originals are in [`brand/`](brand/).

## You can

- Talk about the project, write about it and link to it.
- Use the name and the logos to refer to the project, for example in an article, a review or a list of apps.
- Say that your server "works with Kosmotheka".
- Run the code and change it for yourself, as the licence allows.

## You cannot

- Publish an app, a server or a service, modified or not, **under the Kosmotheka name or with its logos** in a way that makes people think it is the official one.
- Use the name or the logos in a way that suggests the project endorses you.
- Change the logos (colours, shape, proportions) or use them as part of your own logo.

If you publish a fork, give it its own name and its own icon.

## What is official

- The official app is the one on Google Play and the downloads on [kosmotheka.com](https://kosmotheka.com).
- The official server is `api.kosmotheka.app`.

Hosting your own server is fine, and the official app can connect to it. It simply is not "the official Kosmotheka", and the app says so to whoever uses it.
